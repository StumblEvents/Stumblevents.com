Account help

Delete your Stumbl account

Delete in the app

When you can access Stumbl, go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account, then review and confirm the request. Stumbl verifies the signed-in account before processing it.

Request deletion without the app

If you cannot open the app, email support@stumblevents.com with the subject “Account deletion request.” Include the email address or username associated with the account, but do not send passwords, payment-card information, or government ID by ordinary email.

Before acting, Stumbl may request information reasonably necessary to verify that you control the account and to protect against unauthorized deletion. If you have an active ticket, paid event, refund, dispute, safety matter, or another legal or financial obligation, Stumbl records the request and may restrict new activity while it completes the necessary review.

What deletion means

When deletion can be completed, Stumbl removes account credentials and eligible profile, settings, follows, saved-event, notification, image, and user-content data. Some records may be retained where needed for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, financial reconciliation, dispute resolution, or backups. Stumbl does not promise immediate deletion or a fixed completion period.
