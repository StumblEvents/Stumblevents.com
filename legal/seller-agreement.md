# Stumbl Ticket Seller Agreement

**Status:** Published.
**Version:** 2026-11-20-v1
**Effective date:** November 20, 2026

This Agreement applies only to an organizer that enables tickets through Stumbl’s supported checkout (**Seller** or **you**). It supplements the Terms and Event Creator Agreement. It does not apply to free events or external-ticket links. Ticketed-event operations are initially intended for Utah.

## 1. Eligibility, onboarding, and event responsibilities

You must be eligible to contract, have authority to sell tickets, complete required onboarding, and provide accurate identity, payout, tax, and event information. Users under 18 may not sell Stumbl-paid tickets or receive organizer payouts. You are responsible for event delivery, admission, inventory, listing accuracy, consumer communications, permits, venue authority, accessibility, and compliance with the laws applicable where your event occurs. You must not imply that Stumbl organizes, endorses, or guarantees the event.

When Stumbl supports the minor purchase-request flow, the adult is the purchaser and payer for receipt, payment, and refund purposes, while the minor may be the assigned attendee. This does not create duplicate admission credentials or override event age or admission restrictions.

## 2. Checkout, fee, and payment status

For an enabled paid checkout, the buyer must see the ticket price, applicable tax, the Stumbl ticketing fee of **6.5% of the paid ticket subtotal plus $0.99 per checkout**, and the total before purchase. A free-only checkout has no ticketing fee. Stumbl administers ticket payments and refunds through Stripe. This Agreement does not determine merchant-of-record status, tax liability, or regulatory status.

Stumbl collects ticket payments through Stripe. Stumbl transfers eligible net ticket proceeds to your connected Stripe account according to the schedule and hold conditions in Section 3.

## 3. Organizer transfers, holds, and recovery

Stumbl transfers eligible net ticket proceeds to your connected Stripe account in two installments:

1. Initial transfer. The first 85% of eligible net ticket proceeds becomes eligible for transfer no earlier than 72 hours after the confirmed actual end of the event or showing.
2. Reserve release. Stumbl retains the remaining 15% as a reserve. The reserve becomes eligible for transfer no earlier than 14 days after the confirmed actual end of the event or showing. If a dispute remains open at that time, Stumbl retains the portion of the reserve needed to cover the outstanding disputed amount and may release the undisputed remainder, subject to any other applicable holds. Once the dispute is resolved, Stumbl applies any required adjustment and releases any remaining eligible balance.

Both installments are subject to available funds, identity and account verification, and applicable holds for pending eligible refunds, suspected fraud, legal requirements, or payment processor requirements. Stumbl accounts for pending eligible refund requests before initiating a transfer. These timeframes establish when funds become eligible for transfer to your connected Stripe account. The timing of a payout to your bank depends on your Stripe payout schedule and your bank’s processing times. The reserve is not held in escrow.

**Recovery after transfer.** If a refund, chargeback, or dispute adjustment reduces the proceeds you are entitled to after Stumbl has transferred funds to you, you remain responsible for repaying the resulting overpayment. This obligation continues after account deletion. To the extent permitted by law and supported by the payment processor, you authorize Stumbl to recover the amount from your available reserve, offset it against future ticket proceeds, or reverse all or part of the original transfer. If those methods do not cover the amount owed, Stumbl may request repayment of the remaining balance.

## 4. Cancellations, refunds, and deletion

You must honor the Stumbl Terms of Service, the event refund policy shown to the buyer at checkout and recorded at the time of purchase, and applicable law. Changes to your event refund policy apply only to future purchases.

## 5. Enforcement, term, and contact

Authorized admins may deny, pause, remove, or restrict ticket-sales access for incomplete onboarding, safety, fraud, legal, compliance, or repeat-violation risk. Where appropriate, Stumbl may give notice and an opportunity to correct a violation; serious abuse, fraud, urgent safety concerns, repeated violations, or required legal action may lead to immediate action. These obligations survive for completed orders, refunds, disputes, records, and payment obligations. Utah law and Salt Lake County courts apply as stated in the Terms, subject to mandatory law.
